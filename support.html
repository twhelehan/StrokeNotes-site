StrokeNotes Support

Help, feedback and beta testing support for StrokeNotes.

Contact

For help, feedback or beta testing issues, email:

support@strokenotes.co.uk

Beta testers: please include as much detail as possible so issues can be reproduced and fixed quickly.

When reporting a bug

Please include:

  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • The StrokeNotes app version or TestFlight build number
  • What you were doing when the issue happened
  • Whether the issue happens every time or only sometimes
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if useful

Useful feedback

StrokeNotes is being shaped around real rowing use, so feedback from coaches, coxes, rowers and club volunteers is especially useful.

Helpful feedback includes anything that feels confusing, anything missing from the outing log, drill finder or incident reporting flow, and any wording that does not match how rowing clubs actually talk.
