TestFlight beta

Request beta access

StrokeNotes is currently being tested with rowers, coxes, coaches and club volunteers. If you would like to try the app and share feedback, you can request a TestFlight invite.

Join the StrokeNotes beta

Beta testers help shape StrokeNotes by trying the app in realistic rowing settings and reporting what works well, what feels confusing, and what should be improved before a wider release.

To request access, please email info@strokenotes.co.uk.

Please include: your name, club or organisation, rowing role, the iPhone model you use if known, and what you would like to test.

Useful beta testers include

  • Coaches who want to record session plans, feedback and focus areas
  • Coxes who want to keep notes on crews, calls, steering and race pieces
  • Scullers or crew members who want a structured outing history
  • Club volunteers involved in safety, incident reporting or coaching administration
  • Users with telemetry files from NK, CrewNerd, GPX or TCX sources
