StrokeNotes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 June 2026

StrokeNotes is a rowing coaching and session logging app.

Data collected by the app

StrokeNotes allows users to enter rowing session notes, crew information, drills, incidents, and related coaching information.

How data is stored

At this stage, StrokeNotes stores app data locally on the user’s device. The app does not currently require an account and does not upload user data to our own servers.

Email and sharing

If a user chooses to share or export information by email, the content is sent using the user’s chosen email service.

Third-party services

StrokeNotes may use Apple services as part of normal iOS app operation and distribution through TestFlight or the App Store.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@strokenotes.co.uk
