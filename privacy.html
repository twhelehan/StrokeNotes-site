Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 June 2026

Overview

StrokeNotes is a rowing session logging app for coaches, coxes, rowers and rowing clubs.

This policy explains how information is handled by the StrokeNotes app.

Data stored in the app

StrokeNotes allows users to enter rowing session notes, crew names, boat names, drills, incidents, feedback, photos, videos and related information.

This information is stored locally on the user’s device unless the user chooses to share or export it.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data from the app.

StrokeNotes does not currently require an account, login or cloud service operated by us.

Third-party services

StrokeNotes does not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.

If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated.

Email, export and sharing

If a user chooses to export a session, share a report or send an incident email, the information they choose to share will be handled by their email provider or selected sharing method.

Users should check the contents of any exported report or email before sending it.

Photos and videos

If users add photos or videos to StrokeNotes, those files are used only for the purpose of recording or reviewing rowing sessions within the app.

Users are responsible for obtaining any necessary permission before recording or sharing images, video or information about other people.

Children and junior rowers

StrokeNotes may be used in rowing environments where junior rowers are present. Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal information about juniors or other individuals.

Coaches and clubs should follow their own safeguarding, data protection and photography policies when using the app.

Future features

Future versions of StrokeNotes may include optional features such as sync, backup, cloud storage, crash reporting, analytics or integrations with other rowing tools.

If such features are added, this privacy policy and the relevant App Store privacy information will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@strokenotes.co.uk
